Our Story

About Jarvi Nursery

For 14+ years, farmers across India have trusted Jarvi for guava saplings built for real fields, real soil, and real income. Deep red pulp, heavy fruit, premium market price — every season.

Who We Are

Jarvi Seeds & Nursery Pvt. Ltd. is a Gujarat-based nursery specialising in high-yield guava saplings, grown and grafted using our own rootstock technology. We are proud to be India's first NHB (National Horticulture Board) registered nursery in Dragon Fruit.

Our Mission

To give every farmer — from a half-acre plot to a large commercial orchard — access to saplings that are bred for real field conditions, not just for looking good in a nursery. Higher yield, stronger disease resistance, and a better price at market.

Our Reach

Grown in Bharadiya, Valia, Bharuch, Gujarat, and delivered across India. 500+ farmers already trust Jarvi saplings for their orchards.
