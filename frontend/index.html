Know Your Guava

Jarvi Red vs Jarvi Red-1

A detailed look at how our two flagship varieties compare, straight from our own field trial data.

Specification Jarvi Red PPVFR&A Reg. No.: REG/2021/127 Jarvi Red-1 PPVFR&A Reg. No.: REG/2023/164
Year of Development 2008-09 2020-21
Fruit Yield 55 t/ha (Average after 3 year) 60 t/ha (Average after 3 year)
Fruit Shape Rounded Necked (Pear Shape)
Average Fruit Weight 350-400 g 450-500 g
Fruit Pulp Colour Red Purple group Red Purple group
Fruit Pulp Thickness Thick - ~3.00 cm Thick – ~2.8 cm
Fruit Acidity (%) Low (Average – 0.42) Low (Average – 0.34)
TSS (⁰B) 13.0-14.5 (⁰B) 14.0-15.5 (⁰B)
Vitamin C 260-275 mg/100g 280-300 mg/100g
Seeds per 100g Fruit Weight 3-4 seeds 9-12 seeds
Seed Hardiness (Kg/cm²) Soft seed - 12.2 kg/cm² Medium hard seed – 10.3 kg/cm²
Purpose Especially suitable for table purpose Especially suitable for table purpose
Note Main Drawback: fruit setting is less when temperature increases above 34°C. Advantage: no issue regarding fruit setting till 45°C.
